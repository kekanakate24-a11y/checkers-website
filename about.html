About Checkers South Africa

Checkers is a leading supermarket retailer in South Africa, forming a key part of Shoprite Holdings Ltd. We focus on offering consumers premium quality products at affordable prices.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

To deliver an exceptional retail experience by offering fresh food, premium international products, and unbeatable value through our Xtra Savings program and Sixty60 app.

Vision

To remain South Africa's first-choice food retailer through continuous digital innovation and customer-first service.

Our Specialty Brands

  • Simple Truth: Organic, free-from, and eco-friendly products.
  • Forage & Feast: A luxury food range endorsed by Michelin-star chefs.
  • Farmers Market: Farm-fresh fruits, vegetables, and meat sourced directly from producers.
