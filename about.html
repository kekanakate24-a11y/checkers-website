About Checkers South Africa

Checkers is a leading supermarket retailer in South Africa, forming a key part of Shoprite Holdings Ltd. We focus on offering consumers premium quality products at affordable prices.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission

Checkers focuses on enriching the lives of customers and local communities by offering high-quality products at affordable prices. It also highlights commitments to sustainability, supporting local suppliers, and encouraging inclusivity and respect

Vision

To be the most convenient and accessible retailer in South Africa, reflecting its aspiration to provide a seamless and enjoyable shopping experience for all customers.

Our Specialty Brands

  • Simple Truth: Organic, free-from, and eco-friendly products.
  • Forage & Feast: A luxury food range endorsed by Michelin-star chefs.
  • Farmers Market: Farm-fresh fruits, vegetables, and meat sourced directly from producers.
